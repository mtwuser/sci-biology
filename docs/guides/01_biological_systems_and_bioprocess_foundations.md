## 1. Progressive `M1 -> M4` Biology Mastery & Bioprocess Ladder

| Mastery Stage | Cognitive & Scientific Focus | Core Biological Pillars | Verification Gate (`verify-mastery`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`M1` (Foundations)** | Cellular organization, macromolecules (carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids), and binomial taxonomy (`BIO-SCI-01`). | Cell theory, photosynthesis vs. cellular respiration, DNA replication & transcription, trophic chains. | Classify organisms by domain/family and trace carbon/energy flow across cellular compartments. |
| **`M2` (University Rigor)** | Quantitative biochemistry, Michaelis-Menten enzyme kinetics, osmotic water potential ($\Psi_w$), and Mendelian/population genetics (`BIO-SCI-02`). | Enzyme kinetics ($V_{\max}, K_m$), membrane transport, water activity ($a_w$), plant vascular physiology (`xylem`/`phloem`). | Calculate reaction rates, osmotic cut-points ($a_w < 0.60$), and Hardy-Weinberg equilibria. |
| **`M3` (Applied Bioprocess)** | Solid-State Fermentation (`SSF`), lignocellulosic biomaterial stabilization, and biosafety / non-invasiveness (`BIO-SCI-03`). | Moisture stasis (`< 10.0%` w/w), thermal dehydration windows (`[35.0°C, 40.0°C]`), `RD 630/2013` botanical screening (`dept-iso-bio`). | Audit batch records (`Record #NNN`) against `BIO-01..04` and attach `sci-biology` `citation_receipt`s. |
| **`M4` (Frontier Systems & Bioprocess Bridge)** | Systems biology, metabolic flux analysis, biophysics (`sci-physics`), and industrial bioprocess scaling (`sci-engineering` bridge). | Stoichiometric flux balance (`FBA`), coupled reaction-diffusion (`sci-maths`), and biorefinery kinetic scaling. | Author `E3/E2` specifications linking molecular mechanisms to macroscopic ecosystem or bioprocess outcomes. |
