## 1. Water Activity ($a_w$) & Microbial Proliferation Cut-Points (`BIO-SCI-02` — `E3`)

Water activity $a_w = p / p_0$ (ratio of equilibrium vapor pressure of the substrate to pure water at temperature $T$) governs osmotic availability for cellular metabolism:

| Water Activity Cut-Point ($a_w$) | Equilibrium Moisture (`% w/w` Lignocellulose) | Limiting Organism Group | Biological State & Preservation Guarantee |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`a_w >= 0.91`** | `> 22.0%` | Gram-negative & Gram-positive bacteria (*Pseudomonas*, *Escherichia*, *Bacillus*) | Rapid bacterial proliferation and putrefaction. |
| **`0.80 <= a_w < 0.91`** | `15.0% – 22.0%` | Yeasts (*Saccharomyces*) & common molds (*Penicillium*, *Mucor*) | Aerobic fungal sporulation and mycotoxin risk. |
| **`0.60 <= a_w < 0.80`** | `10.0% – 15.0%` | Xerophilic fungi (*Aspergillus restrictus*) & osmophilic yeasts | Slow xerophilic enzymatic degradation. |
| **`a_w < 0.60` (`Cut-Point`)** | **`< 10.0% w/w`** | **Zero microbial proliferation** | **Absolute Microbial & Fungal Stasis (`dept-iso-bio` `BIO-01`)** — DNA/RNA replication and cytoplasmic translation cease completely. |

## 2. Lignocellulosic Biopolymer Fractions & Thermal Stability Windows (`BIO-SCI-01..02` — `E3`)

Plant cell walls consist of three primary structural biopolymers with distinct chemical and thermal transitions (`sci-physics` `PHYS-03`):

1. **Cellulose ($\text{(C}_6\text{H}_{10}\text{O}_5\text{)}_n$, `35% – 50%` dry mass)**: Linear $\beta(1 \to 4)$-linked D-glucose crystalline microfibrils; thermal depolymerization onset `300 °C – 380 °C`.
2. **Hemicellulose (`20% – 35%` dry mass)**: Branched heteropolysaccharides (xylan, glucomannan); reactive cross-linking and thermal cleavage window `200 °C – 300 °C`.
3. **Lignin (`15% – 30%` dry mass)**: Cross-linked aromatic phenylpropane polymer (*p-coumaryl*, *coniferyl*, *sinapyl* alcohols) providing hydrophobicity and structural rigidity; gradual carbonization across `250 °C – 500 °C`.
4. **Enzymatic & Structural Preservation Window (`[35.0 °C, 40.0 °C]`)**: Dehydration within `35.0 °C – 40.0 °C` preserves native pectin/lignocellulosic matrix integrity without triggering Maillard browning or protein denaturation (`> 42.0 °C`, `BIO-02`).
